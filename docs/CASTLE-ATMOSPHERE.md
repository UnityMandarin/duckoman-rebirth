# Castle atmosphere and midpoint checkpoints

The first castle level reuses the illustrated arch, banner and foreground pillar artwork from the jail pass. Occasional royal parchment scrolls provide the falling detail. The original castle painting, terrain, controls and combat remain in place.

The castle has one checkpoint on the existing platform around x=5725, close to its horizontal midpoint (5760). The jail keeps only its middle checkpoint near x=8720. Outdoor checkpoints are unchanged. Checkpoints are run-local and require physical proximity; reloading the page does not persist progress.
