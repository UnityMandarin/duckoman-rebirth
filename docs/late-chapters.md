# Chapters 6 and 7

Stormworks Citadel and Crown’s Edge are separate traversal runners built from the existing Player, input, HUD, cake, enemy, interaction, checkpoint, and trap systems. They add no new physics or damage rules. Each chapter has twelve 1,440-unit rooms (17,280 units total) and ten authored traversal steps per room. Stormworks therefore has a 53% higher step-density proxy than Furnace (79 steps in 15,840 units); that describes authored traversal-action density only, not measured completion time or difficulty. Optional routes and moving platforms mean the proxy does not imply every node is mandatory.

The backgrounds and decorative art are reused from the repository: `world-v3-crimson-works` for Stormworks, `world-v3-castle-audience` for Crown’s Edge, the existing `identity-jail` atlas for platform faces and chain supports, and Furnace utility/climax frames for machinery warnings, the signal beacon, and the completion panel. No new raster art was generated. Background images keep their source proportions and repeat on a single, floorless chapter-wide field; movement collisions come only from authored platforms.

The route tests exercise every static edge at 30, 60, and 120 Hz, sample lift/ferry motion phases, evaluate conveyor drift at the trap system’s capped 50 ms step, and sample shutter collision availability against the runtime’s warning/gone/recovery timings. These are route-level simulation checks, not a full human playthrough. The local-only `late-qa.html` fixture stages the original Player on a real support and exposes input-driven movement plus live telemetry; it does not write campaign saves.
