# Chapter identity art

Four independent transparent sprite atlases were generated for the jail, outside journey, Crimson Kingdom, and rescue. The four source PNGs are preserved byte-for-byte at their native 1254×1254 RGBA size. The atlas frame rectangles are in [`public/assets/identity/frames.json`](../public/assets/identity/frames.json), in native image pixels as `[x, y, width, height]`.

## Measurement and alpha

Non-foundation crops are tightly measured to the sprite silhouette in a manually selected full-image region, using alpha values greater than 32. This avoids faint stray alpha and keeps adjacent sprites out of the crop. Platform and carrier crops focus on the actual deck; hanging chains, rope rigging, and overhead noise are excluded where they extend above the collision surface. The jail spikes point downward in the source image and require `flipY: true` when rendered; the other three spikes face upward as authored.

Each foundation crop is the largest inner rectangular material region whose source pixels all have alpha between 250 and 254. The PNGs remain untouched. The renderer should place an opaque backing beneath the foundation material to cover the remaining alpha variation.

A final overlap audit clipped affected measurements to the intended object regions before recalculating alpha-greater-than-32 bounds: jail shutter starts at y=410; outside latch starts at y=940; Crimson door frame ends at y=894, belt ends at y=474, rest marker starts at x=1034, and crusher press ends at x=935; rescue door frame ends at y=917. The resulting final rectangles are recorded in `frames.json`.

All four native images are 1254×1254 RGBA with alpha extrema 0–255. SHA-256 values confirm the project copies match their generated sources:
