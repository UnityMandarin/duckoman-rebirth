# Royal archive landmark

Two new concepts—a royal duck statue holding cake and a web-footed scroll cabinet—were developed into one complete painted archive alcove. The finished opaque scene is integrated behind the castle's opening gameplay area, with restrained parallax and its painted floor aligned to the collision floor. The first repeated arch is omitted to keep the landmark readable. No geometry, collisions, hazards or controls are changed.

The isolated concept-sheet exports did not provide usable alpha, so they are not shipped as cutouts. `public/assets/depth/royal-archive.png` is a complete intentional opaque painting, not a transparent placeholder. Generated with the built-in image tool; no model version claimed.

Initial concept prompt:

Production 2D game-art cutout sheet for Duckoman Rebirth, TWO separate illustrated objects on genuine transparent RGBA background. Square canvas, left half and right half, keep objects separated with generous transparent gap and padding. LEFT HALF: one memorable old royal duck statue, a proudly round crowned duck holding a tiny ceremonial cake on a cushion, carved cool blue stone with worn antique gold crown and beak, broad chamfered pedestal with a carved feather emblem, funny but dignified silhouette, whole statue and pedestal visible. RIGHT HALF: one tall royal archive stand: old warm oak pigeonhole cabinet with six irregular compartments holding rolled ivory scrolls tied with faded blue ribbons, a brass duck-head finial, a small unlit blue glass lantern hanging from side hook, carved little webbed feet supporting the cabinet. Cohesive painterly broad hand-shaped brush planes, slate-blue shadows and muted old gold edge lighting from upper left. Match the attached royal-prison art's palette and shape language, but no chains or prison symbols on the new props. Strong readable silhouettes and purposeful wear, no random noise, no glow or particles, no backdrop, no floor, no cast external shadow, no text, NO drawn checkerboard or white matte. These are ready-to-use actual game props, not a scene or concept board.

Final integrated painting prompt:

Turn these two reference prop designs into ONE complete wide landscape game background painting. A royal Duckoman archive alcove, side-view: the blue-stone crowned duck statue holding cake stands on the left; the warm-oak scroll cabinet with duck head finial and webbed feet stands on the right, both grounded on the same thin stone floor at 92 percent canvas height. Between them a large QUIET recessed slate-blue wall with faint stone feather relief and generous empty central area for gameplay. Stone arched ceiling and framing columns, antique brass accents, soft cool overhead daylight, restrained warm reflected light near archive shelves, chunky painterly shapes consistent with reference. Actual complete opaque background scene, no transparency, NO CHECKERBOARD whatsoever, no UI, no text, no characters, no particles or glow. Wide 16:9 composition. Very dark navy outer framing edges and corners blend into a shadowed castle. Props medium-sized, together occupy at most half of scene width, distant rather than foreground, muted contrast so bright yellow Duckoman and hazards will read clearly in front. Purposeful royal humor and handcrafted painterly composition. Do not copy another game's design.
