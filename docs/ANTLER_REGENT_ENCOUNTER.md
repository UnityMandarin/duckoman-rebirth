# Antler Regent encounter

The Regent has 20 HP and enters Royal Fury at 10 HP. Dash, stomp, cake, and ultimate routes keep their existing damage, including four damage from an ultimate and half a heart from a hazard contact. Ordinary nonlethal hits do not change an attack, its cue clock, combo order, or hazards. The first hit that crosses 10 HP starts one 1.2-second Royal Fury roar, clears hazards, and grounds the Regent; later hits leave that roar timer intact. Royal Fury then uses fixed attack groups: charge→sweep, slam→thorns, charge, and thorns→sweep. Horizontal sweep reach, thorn width, wave width, and charge bounds are each three times their previous size; their vertical bands and all movement speeds remain unchanged. Charge and sweep cues cover their arena-clipped damage bounds, including the charge's conservative projected travel. A pooled floor-band image makes the expanded charge footprint visible. Each group ends with at least 1.1 seconds of recovery, with a longer recovery after a charge wall impact. Floor thorns keep their alternating anchors, with one additional 156-pixel eruption captured on a nearby ledge when the player's feet and body overlap it, clipped to the available ledge. Hazards use arena-clipped world rectangles and retain 700 ms invulnerability behavior. Hit tint and pooled impact art last 190 ms.

The original player physics were measured through real `Player.update` and Arcade Body update/post-update at 30, 60, and 120 Hz. Normal-jump apex was 112.47, 117.375, and 119.8275 pixels; airtime was about 0.8 seconds; feet stayed at least 64 pixels above the floor for 0.50, 0.55, and 0.55 seconds. A 192-pixel wave moving at 240 pixels per second occupies a crossing lane for 800 ms. Sweep and charge retain their 64-pixel floor band. These figures validate geometry and timing against measured physics; they do not claim a full playthrough or user playtest.

Pose and defeat atlases are loaded as native Phaser frames from `public/assets/bosses/regent/manifest.json`. Effects are pooled. The generated grove plate sits behind the existing collision floor and ledges; no arena geometry or player tuning changed. Encounter camera zoom and position ease across both actors, scale with the world render multiplier, and restore the player-follow camera before progression callbacks.
